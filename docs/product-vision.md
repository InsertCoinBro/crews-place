# Product vision

Crew's Place will be an inviting world where autistic children and young adults can choose their own path through games, creative activities, learning, movement, and calming experiences.

The experience should support different ages, communication styles, motor abilities, reading levels, and sensory needs. The player—not a timer or score—sets the pace.

## Early activity families

- Create: coloring, music, building, and storytelling
- Play: matching, sorting, puzzles, memory, and cause-and-effect
- Learn: letters, numbers, emotions, daily living, and communication
- Move: optional movement breaks and imitation activities
- Calm: breathing, visual rhythms, gentle soundscapes, and sensory play

## First milestone

Build one polished activity hub and one small game. Use that first slice to establish navigation, settings, feedback, and the reusable game pattern before adding more paths.
